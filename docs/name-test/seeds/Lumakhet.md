# Launch announcement: Lumakhet

Lumakhet is a new desktop web browser, announced today by an independent developer. It will be a free download for macOS first, with Windows and Linux builds to follow. It is built on Chromium, so ordinary websites load normally.

## What Lumakhet does

Search results appear as a mind map instead of a list of links. A person clicks anywhere and asks a question. Lumakhet draws the question in the middle, with websites, news, research papers and discussions around it, and each source can be opened from the map. AI summaries are labeled as AI, and their citations are checked against the source text.

Pages are drawn on a curved screen. Tabs can surround the person in a 360° room on an ordinary laptop screen, with no headset, or float as glass windows that can be grouped.

Lumakhet includes a station for AI agents. A person creates an agent in three steps: choose a character, give it a mission, choose its powers. Agents gain experience and ranks, and each kind of work plays its own visual effect. Agents can research the web, collect lists and tables into spreadsheets, crawl up to 40 pages while obeying each site's robots.txt, summarize a person's Gmail, read a YouTube video's details, and build small apps that run with no internet access.

An Android phone can be seen and controlled inside a window, and an iPhone can be viewed. Ads and trackers are blocked by default. Synced data is end-to-end encrypted.

## What people pay

The browser is free. Agents run on the person's own AI key (Claude), billed by the AI company per use. There is no Lumakhet subscription, and a person who doesn't want to pay can use the map without AI.

## Limits at launch

Agents will not sign in, enter passwords or card details, buy anything, send email, or solve CAPTCHAs. They read and prepare, and the person acts. Chrome extensions and a built-in password manager are not available. Netflix and Spotify playback do not work. The app is not yet code-signed, so macOS shows a security warning on first launch. It comes from one developer, not a large company.
