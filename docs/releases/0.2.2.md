# AetherDeck 0.2.2 for Mac (preview) — draft release notes

**AetherDeck for Mac** is a browser whose window is your **Pod**: your real tabs hang on a curved ring around you, with a 360° view outside, and AetherDeck's home, Cosmonaut, agents and research in the first pane. Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

## Install

1. Download `AetherDeck-0.2.2-mac-arm64.dmg`, open it and drag **AetherDeck** into Applications.
2. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet. The first time, open it, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
3. A short setup walks you through your profile, AI, Google, search, ads, the dock and a tour of the Pod. Every step can be skipped.

SHA-256 of the disk image: `ab95e89bb21921ed150758d808f3333374b4c7d66b86c44385c6d28614510d64`

## What's new since 0.2.0

- **Sharp pages.** When the view rests on a pane, that pane shows the live page at your screen's own resolution, like a normal tab, with native scrolling, text selection and pointer. Turning shows the bent picture again.
- **Smoother.** While you turn, only the pane you're on is redrawn. Measured with 8 live panes (YouTube, BBC, GitHub, Wikipedia and others) on a 16 GB M-series Mac: 48–59 frames a second while turning, 59 at rest, CPU about half of one core at rest.
- **Eight panes, all live on 16 GB Macs**, and a **More tabs (n)** box for the rest: swap one in for the pane you're facing, or drag it onto a pane.
- **Built-in ad and tracker blocking** (uBlock Origin, on by default, nothing to install). You can switch it off in the setup or in Add-ons.
- **First-run setup:** a local profile (name and picture, on this Mac only), AI, Google sign-in on Google's own page, search engine, ad blocking, your dock, a tour of the Pod, importing from Safari, Chrome or Firefox, and the default browser.
- **Profile card** on the avatar: see and edit your name and picture.
- **AI your way:** any API key, Ollama with its address, any OpenAI-compatible service, or **Sign in with ChatGPT** (Plus or Pro).
- **Widgets** sit flat in a row in front of the Pod: drag to move, pull the corner to resize, × to hide, double-click to reset. **Agents can make widgets** (a clock, a checklist, a data panel) and place them in the Pod; each runs sandboxed with no network, storage or access to your pages, and you can view its code or delete it.
- **Clicks that didn't work now do:** Firefox's own pages and dialogs inside a pane (Settings, Import), and a page's own dialogs (the Pod steps aside while one is open).
- The pointer shows the right shape (hand over links, text bar over text) and is never hidden.
- Groups of sites open as Pod panes; any new tab joins the Pod; the top-bar Pod button brings you back in.
- Voice: the microphone no longer waits on a hidden prompt; quiet voices register; recording stops after 8 seconds of silence.

## Known limits

- **Unsigned:** the first launch needs **Open Anyway** (above). No automatic updates yet.
- **Mac only, Apple silicon only.** Windows and Linux stay on the earlier AetherDeck app (0.1.1).
- **Safari import** needs one step from you: macOS keeps Safari's data private, so the import window asks you to pick the Safari folder (or give AetherDeck Full Disk Access in System Settings).
- **Some sites may block us.** Lenovo, for example, showed "Access Denied" in testing; its bot protection judges browsers it doesn't recognise. That's the site's choice, not something AetherDeck blocks.
- **YouTube ads** are blocked most of the time, but YouTube changes its tricks every few weeks and ads can slip through until the filters update.
- A short pause (about 50–65 ms) when the view settles on a pane and swaps in the live page.
- Agent-made widgets can't save their own state yet (an agent updates them instead).
- Dictation in the search box isn't available in this browser; use the microphone button for agents, or a dictation app such as OpenWhispr.
