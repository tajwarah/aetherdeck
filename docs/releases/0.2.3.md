# AetherDeck 0.2.3 for Mac (preview)

**AetherDeck for Mac** is a browser whose window is your **Pod**: your real tabs hang on a curved ring around you, with a 360° view outside, and AetherDeck's home, Cosmonaut, agents and research in the first pane. Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

## Install

1. Download `AetherDeck-0.2.3-mac-arm64.dmg`, open it and drag **AetherDeck** into Applications.
2. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet. The first time, open it, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

SHA-256 of the disk image: `abb7c7a9f2dbd7cf8f0b3274d25411de95bd0c585901707c188230143050d123`

## What's new since 0.2.2

- **Smooth, measured.** With YouTube 1080p60 playing and 8 panes open, the Pod now runs at 58–60 frames a second at rest and while turning, about the same as a normal tab. 0.2.2 ran at 27–55. How:
  - a pane's picture is redrawn only when its page changes;
  - panes beside you are redrawn at a modest rate;
  - nothing is redrawn while you turn;
  - panes behind you rest, but anything playing audio or video keeps playing.
- **Bigger, sharper text in panes.** Pages in the Pod are shown at 125% (reading zoom), so text and the text cursor are easier to see.
- **Panes never overlap.** Fixed the seam of the ring where the last pane met the first.
- **Full screen stays in its pane.** A video's own ⛶ button fills its pane in place: no black frame, no popup, and Esc comes back.
  - **Fit** (or the F key) still fills the whole view, Theater-style, with the Pod around it.
- **Cinema (preview).** Put any tab on a cinema screen in a quiet theatre; nothing else is drawn, so it costs about what a normal tab does.
  - Streaming services (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Max, Hulu, Apple TV+ and others) and any page playing protected video are offered the Cinema. Their video can't show on a bent pane.
  - You choose once per site ("Remember for this site").
- **Cosmonaut opens as a side panel** on the right of the home, never under the dock, with a short slide-in. The agents' chat too.
- **Widgets are out of the way.** They live on a shelf at the left edge, closed by default. They never sit in the middle of the view or on top of each other; a Reset button puts them back; flat glass instead of blur.
- **The Pod's bar works at any window width.** Buttons no longer squeeze or fall off narrow windows (the bar wraps onto two rows), and the **Tabs** list opens again.
- **More tabs** counts tabs opened in the background straight away.
- **Settings and Import:** when Firefox opens a dialog in a pane (Import, for example), the Pod turns to it.
- The home's Earth turns only when the home pane is in front.

## Known limits

- **Unsigned:** the first launch needs **Open Anyway** (above). No automatic updates yet.
- **Mac only, Apple silicon only.** Windows and Linux stay on the earlier AetherDeck app (0.1.1).
- **Protected video** (Netflix and similar) can play in the front pane, Theater or Cinema, never in a bent pane. Some services may also refuse this build until it carries Google's browser signing for Widevine.
- **By default your tabs are not restored** after quitting. Turn on "Open previous windows and tabs" in Settings to restore them.
- Panes are still fairly small with 8 open (about a third of the window each): use + / the size slider, Fit or Cinema for a bigger view.
- **Safari import** needs one step from you: macOS keeps Safari's data private, so the import window asks you to pick the Safari folder (or give AetherDeck Full Disk Access in System Settings).
- Some sites may block unfamiliar browsers; YouTube ads can occasionally slip through until uBlock Origin's filters update.
