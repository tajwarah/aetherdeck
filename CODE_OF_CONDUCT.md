# Code of Conduct

AetherDeck is for everyone. We want its issues, discussions and pull requests to be welcoming, whoever you are and
however much experience you have.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and questions. Everyone was new once.
- Give credit where it's due.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who keep
breaking it. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile,
[@tajwarah](https://github.com/tajwarah), or privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are kept
confidential.

This code applies in every AetherDeck space, and when someone represents the project in public.
