# Brand assets

- `earth-band.webp` and `earth-night.webp`: Earth at night from NASA's Black Marble ("The earth at night", NASA Earth
  Observatory / Suomi NPP VIIRS, public domain; via Wikimedia Commons). The band holds latitudes 72°N to 12°S for the
  turning globe; the picture is the home's view, shown until the globe is drawn. The horizon glow is drawn on top.
- `logo.png`, `icon.png`: the AetherDeck mark, the brand's own artwork, cut from its dark ground.
- Lexend Exa (`public/fonts/`): The Lexend Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`public/fonts/OFL.txt`).
